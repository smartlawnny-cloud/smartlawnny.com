Your problem is the opposite of the tree company's.

Likely Here marketing genuinely moves the needle — but not because you need more posts. You're selling a new category: most Westchester homeowners don't know a wire-free robot can mow their whole yard. The job is education + local trust, because the fear ("does it work on my slope? who fixes it?") is what kills the sale, and that fear is exactly what an online box-seller can't answer and you can.

03 · The growth engine

The landscaper channel is the flywheel. Retail proof feeds it.

Chasing homeowners one at a time is slow in a category nobody's heard of. But a landscaper who already has trust with 50 homeowners can put your mowers on their lawns — and every installed unit is a working demo the neighbors see.

The fleet flywheel
  1. Sign one landscaper (Lucente is the template) → fleet sale + recurring service contract.
  2. Their crews install your units across the properties they maintain → dozens of live demos in the best neighborhoods.
  3. Neighbors see a silent robot mowing a perfect lawn → they ask who → homeowner leads flow back to you.
  4. Those retail installs become the proof (video, reviews) that closes the next landscaper. Loop widens.

Likely One landscaper is worth 20 cold homeowners of effort. Priority #1: turn the Lucente conversation into a signed fleet + a repeatable landscaper pitch you can run 10 more times.

05 · Content pillars

Education over hype. Show it working, on a real lawn.

"Does it actually work?" proof

Real installs on real Westchester lawns — the robot mowing, the before/after stripe, the slope it climbed. Kills the #1 doubt. This is 40% of everything.

Education: why daily robotic mowing wins

Whisper-quiet vs 95dB gas, zero-emissions (+ the local gas-equipment rules), healthier turf via daily ⅓-cut. Teach the category you're selling into.

The map tool as lead magnet

"Enter your address, see which mower fits + what it costs." Push it relentlessly — it's your best qualifier and competitors don't have it.

Trade-in & local-service trust

The 30-day trade-in, local install, real service. The reasons to buy from you and not a box online.
